Job Title: Senior DevOps Engineer

1. Design, build, and maintain Kubernetes clusters across multiple cloud regions
2. Own CI/CD pipelines end-to-end (GitHub Actions, ArgoCD) for a 50+ microservice platform

Requirements:
- 5+ years operating Kubernetes in production at scale
- Deep expertise in Terraform / Pulumi and multi-cloud infrastructure-as-code
- Strong experience with observability stacks (Prometheus, Grafana, Datadog)
- Proven on-call incident-response experience with postmortem culture
- Bonus: experience with service mesh (Istio/Linkerd) and progressive delivery
